Stopped by Police

LIVE GUIDE

Stay calm, composed, and verify statutory protocol. Constitutional protection is active from initial contact.

Article 22 • CrPC 41A

Know your instant immunity safeguards

Non-violent procedure demands civil conduct from both citizen and officer.

Mandatory DOs

LEGAL OBLIGATIONS
verified

Ask calmly for the officer's name, rank, and police station.

Officers on duty are mandated to wear visible name badges (D.K. Basu Guidelines).
verified

Show digital driving license/RC via DigiLocker or mParivahan.

Physical copies cannot be forcefully demanded under Rule 139 of Central Motor Vehicles Rules & Section 139A IT Act.
verified

Demand a physical or digital receipt/e-challan for any fine.

Never surrender unrecorded cash. An on-the-spot compounding receipt is your right under Section 200 MV Act.
verified

Women can only be searched by a female officer with strict decency.

Mandated strictly by Section 51(2) CrPC. Male personnel are prohibited from physical search of women.
close

Critical DON'Ts

PROHIBITED RISKS
cancel

Do not hand over your physical smartphone or let them browse personal chats.

Phone search requires judicial warrant or explicit cyber-crime investigation seizure order. Article 21 Privacy right applies.
cancel

Do not pay cash without receiving an official printed/e-challan immediately.

Paying unofficial settlements is an offense. Traffic police without authorized compounding machines cannot take cash.
cancel

Do not resist physically; record audio/video politely from a safe distance.

Citizens have the legal right to record police conducting public duty in public spaces, provided you do not obstruct their work.
cancel

Never sign blank papers or statements under pressure.

Any confession given to a police officer is inadmissible as evidence in court under Section 25 of the Indian Evidence Act.

CrPC Sec 41, 50, 51; Motor Vehicles Act 2019; Supreme Court Judgment in D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal guidelines.
