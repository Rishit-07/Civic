## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for a civic legal empowerment platform delivering constitutional rights, statutory awareness, and procedural justice guidelines to citizens across India. The brand personality balances uncompromising institutional authority with radical accessibility, warmth, and composure. Legal matters frequently induce panic, friction, and anxiety; the UI counterbalances this with clean, reassuring, and grounded aesthetics that make statutory rights feel concrete, actionable, and navigable.

The design movement synthesizes **Neo-Constructivism** with **Minimalist Editorial Precision**:
- **Structured Geometry & Architectural Grounding:** Employs precise geometric accents (quarter-circles, concentric rings, isometric anchors) inspired by civic architecture—scales, arches, thresholds, and platforms.
- **Graphic Contrast:** Combines deep evergreen-slate and pure stark black against warm off-white canvas spaces, punctured by an electric vivid orange accent for active engagement and state guidance.
- **Humanist Tactility:** Balances clean vector isometric line art with rounded, pill-shaped tactile controls that signal immediate safety and unhindered agency.
- **Accessibility & Clarity:** Replaces dense legal jargon with structured hierarchy, clear typography, and unambiguous Do/Don't dichotomies.

## Colors

The palette relies on high-contrast civic clarity paired with dynamic spatial cues:

- **Primary Accent (`#FF5A00`):** Vivid judicial orange. Used purposefully for active state pills, progress indicators, interactive focal points, highlight callouts, and isometric spot illustrations.
- **Secondary / Heading Ink (`#17261F`):** Deep botanical slate-black. Imparts statutory weight and organic authority to bold uppercase display headings, primary actionable pills, and solid iconography.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAFAFA`):** Warm chalk off-white providing paper-like warmth, avoiding harsh pure-white glare. Supported by structural grey-tinted organic blobs (`#E8E9F0`) and muted border tones (`#E2E4EB`).
- **Body Neutral (`#111111`):** Deep charcoal body ink, delivering AAA contrast for legal statutes, procedural steps, and reading comfort.
- **Muted Subtext (`#8A8A8A`):** Controlled neutral for right-aligned metadata, section headers, and category tags.
- **Feedback & Compliance:**
  - *Do / Affirmative Action:* Deep Forest Green (`#15803D`) with tint container (`#ECFDF5`).
  - *Don't / Emergency Alert:* Crimson Red (`#DC2626` / `#EF4444`) with protective alert container (`#FEF2F2`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear distinction between statutory weight and functional readability:

- **Display & Headings (Montserrat ExtraBold / Bold):** Rendered in uppercase by default to mimic legal tablets, architectural declarations, and civic wayfinding. Paired with wide letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to ensure legibility when set in deep `#17261F`.
- **Body & Procedural Explanations (Plus Jakarta Sans Regular / Medium):** Friendly, rounded, and humanist sans-serif providing maximum comfort during dense reading. Line height is tuned high (`1.57` to `1.625`) to prevent cognitive exhaustion.
- **Labels, Trackers & Subtitles (Montserrat SemiBold / Bold):** Condensed, upper-cased, and often right-aligned or paired with geometric icons to guide reader eyes to procedural codes, IPC/BNS statutory references, and status tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an airy vertical rhythm designed for step-by-step reading and guided onboarding cards:

- **Mobile Viewport (Primary):** Employs a single-column layout flanked by generous `1.25rem` outer margins. Content blocks, illustrations, and actions stack sequentially with `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) gaps, providing ample breathing room for background abstract geometric forms.
- **Tablet / Responsive Grid:** Scales to an 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Interactive cards sit adjacent to contextual diagrams and legal references.
- **Desktop Grid:** Adapts into a constrained 12-column layout (max-width `1140px`) centered on canvas, ensuring editorial hierarchy does not stretch across wide displays.
- **Spatial Accents:** Architectural background assets (blobs `#E8E9F0`, quarter-circles `#FF5A00`, hairline rings) ignore component boundaries, anchoring the top and bottom edges of viewports to maintain visual dynamism without disrupting typographic reading lines.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids traditional heavy dropshadows in favor of crisp architectural stacking, isometric dimensional layering, and low-contrast surface envelopes:

- **Flat Graphic Hierarchy:** Canvas `#FAFAFA` acts as the primary plane. Secondary cards and surface trays sit on pure white `#FFFFFF` with ultra-fine borders (`1px solid #ECEEF2`) to define crisp perimeters.
- **Organic Depth Planes:** Underlay blobs (`#E8E9F0`) and vivid geometric shapes (`#FF5A00`) render beneath content blocks at `z-index: 0`, creating atmospheric depth without blurring.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets:** Elevated navigation docks and urgent rights cards utilize an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(23, 38, 31, 0.08)`, gently lifting interactive elements while preserving graphic clarity.
- **Interactive State Elevation:** Active touch targets do not rise with heavy shadows; instead, they communicate state changes through high-contrast invert fills (`#111111` to `#FF5A00`) or stroke expansions.

## Shapes

The shape system leverages intentional duality: **stark circular/pill curvature for touch controls** against **precise geometric framing for content containers**:

- **Capsule & Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Buttons, chips, category tags, bottom floating bars, and status badges are rendered with full pill profiles. This induces psychological safety, inviting immediate touch without hesitation.
- **Structural Card Enclosures (`rounded-2xl` / `1.5rem` to `2rem`):** Content cards, advisory panels, and interactive bottom sheets utilize soft-radii corners, harmonizing with background organic blobs.
- **Geometric Pure Shapes:** Circular action buttons (`48px` or `56px` circles), half-circle footers, concentric hairline rings, and solid orange circular dots represent completion checkpoints and navigational compass points.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Pill:** Solid `#111111` or `#17261F` filled pill (`height: 52px`), containing centered white uppercase text (`Montserrat Bold 13px`, tracking `0.08em`). Padding: `0 28px`.
- **Circular Directional Button:** Dedicated round action button (`52px × 52px`, `border-radius: 9999px`), solid black fill `#111111`, housing an optical centered white linear arrow pointing right.
- **Secondary Outlined Pill:** Transparent background with `1.5px solid #111111` outline, black bold uppercase typography.
- **Urgent Rights Trigger:** Crimson filled pill (`#DC2626`) reserved strictly for legal emergencies (e.g., *POLICE DETENTION SOS*, *ARREST PROTOCOL*).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Small pill tags (`height: 26px`, horizontal padding `12px`), light neutral fill (`#F0F1F5`) or orange-tinted fill (`#FFF2EB`), displaying uppercase `label-sm` text.
- **IPC / Legal Reference Tags:** Mono-style outlined chips referencing statutory codes (e.g., *SECTION 41A CrPC*), paired with high-contrast slate text.

### Do & Don't Split Cards
- **Structure:** Dual parallel containers or split-half stacked cards.
- **Do Panel:** Left border accent (`3px solid #15803D`), pale mint background tint (`#F0FDF4`), checkmark badge, and crisp instructions.
- **Don't Panel:** Left border accent (`3px solid #DC2626`), pale rose background tint (`#FEF2F2`), cross badge, and prohibited actions.

### Step-by-Step Procedural Cards
- **Design:** Pure white cards (`rounded-2xl`, border `1px solid #ECEEF2`) featuring isometric spot illustrations (arches, hourglasses, scales, cubes), bold step counters (`01`, `02`), uppercase headline in `#17261F`, and right-aligned subtext.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Fields:** Clean pill or `rounded-xl` containers with subtle borders (`1.5px solid #DCE0E5`), transitioning to `#FF5A00` or `#17261F` on focus.
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes:** Circular selectors (`24px`). Unchecked has neutral border; checked state turns `#17261F` or `#FF5A00` with solid white check or dot interior.

### Illustration Guidelines
- Isometric black line art (`1.5px` to `2px` stroke weight) integrated with solid flat `#FF5A00` accent fills, white spatial blocks, and organic floral or botanical accents to signify growth, justice, and clarity.
