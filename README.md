## 📌 About CIVIC

**CIVIC** is a comprehensive civic legal companion and statutory defense platform built specifically for Indian citizens. It equips individuals with immediate, verified legal guidance, procedural safeguards, and emergency dispatch tools during high-stakes real-world interactions—including traffic stops, police encounters, unlawful detention, search and seizure, and regulatory filings.

Following India's landmark criminal law overhaul, CIVIC reflects all active statutes, bridging historic codes with the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS, 2023)**, **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS, 2023)**, **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA, 2023)**, the **Constitution of India**, and the **Motor Vehicles Act (1988/2019)**.

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

### 🤖 1. AI Law & Order Legal Assistant (`/ask`)
- **Conversational Constitutional AI**: Powered by Google Gemini and real-time civic retrieval engines to deliver legally-grounded answers with section citations.
- **Multimodal Document & Challan Scanner**: Upload or capture photos of police notices, traffic challans, summons, or legal documents for instant procedural breakdown and rights verification.
- **Voice Dictation & Audio Queries**: Speak queries hands-free in high-stress situations with integrated speech recognition and audio playback.
- **Interactive Triage Prompts**: Quick-reply recommendation chips for immediate incident handling (e.g., *"Can police seize my phone?"*, *"Night arrest rules for women"*, *"How to file Zero FIR"*).

### 🛡️ 2. 28+ Offline Scenario Cards & Statutory Precedents (`/scenarios`)
- **Actionable Guidance**: Step-by-step DOs and DON'Ts for high-pressure situations such as unwarranted searches, vehicle impoundment, detention, and bail hearings.
- **Statutory Legal Basis**: Authoritative citations to active sections of BNSS, BNS, the Constitution, and landmark Supreme Court precedents (*D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal*, *Arnesh Kumar*, etc.).
- **Emergency Verification Checklists**: Interactive checklists to audit procedural compliance during arrests, searches, and seizures.

### 🖼️ 3. Parallax Protocol Showcase & Smooth Gallery (`/showcase`)
- **Cinematic Protocol Carousel**: Visual walkthrough highlighting core citizen safeguards:
  - *Traffic Checkpoint Safeguards* (Section 130/139 MVA & DigiLocker validity)
  - *Arrest Safeguards* (BNSS Section 35 / CrPC 41A, Memo of Arrest, Medical examination)
  - *Zero FIR Mandates* (BNSS Section 173(1) jurisdictional universal registration)
  - *Digital Privacy & Device Protection* (Article 21 & 20(3) self-incrimination safeguards)
  - *Night Arrest Restrictions for Women* (BNSS Section 43(5) sunset-to-sunrise restrictions)
- **High-Performance Momentum Scrolling**: Engineered with smooth viewport listeners and responsive card transitions across mobile and desktop.

### 🆔 4. Citizen Profile & Digital QR Emergency Pass (`/profile`, `/verify`)
- **Verifiable Citizen Identity**: Encrypted profile management storing blood group, medical notes, emergency contacts, and civic verification status.
- **Offline Digital QR Pass**: Instant scannable QR code displaying identity, critical medical data, and ICE (In Case of Emergency) contacts without requiring active network connectivity.

### 🔔 5. Real-Time Civic Alerts & Statutory Advisories (`/alerts`)
- **Advisory Feed**: Broadcast legal advisories, high-court rulings, transport department notifications, and consumer rights updates.
- **Urgent Broadcasts**: Pinned statutory banners alerting citizens of procedural changes or statewide emergency notices.

### 🆘 6. Emergency Dispatch & SOS GPS Beacon (`/help`)
- **One-Touch Emergency Helplines**: Immediate dispatch calling for National Emergency (`112`), Women's Helpline (`1090` / `1091`), Legal Aid NALSA (`15100`), Cyber Crime (`1930`), and Senior Citizens (`14567`).
- **Emergency GPS Beacon**: Instant location coordinate retrieval and SMS/WhatsApp emergency dispatch with a single tap.

### 📚 7. 16+ Authoritative Directory & Statutory Reference Screens
- **Constitution & BNS Reference**: Fundamental Rights (Articles 14, 19, 20, 21, 22, 32).
- **CrPC to BNSS Transition Engine**: Real-time cross-reference tables between repealed codes and active 2023 statutes.
- **Court & Legal Aid Directories**: Access points for District Legal Services Authorities (DLSA), State Legal Services Authorities (SLSA), and high court legal clinics.
- **Specialized Rights Portals**: Dedicated modules for Police Encounters, FIR Registration, Digital Rights, and Verified Advocates.

### 📝 8. Incident Notes & Tamper-Proof Evidence Vault (`/notes`)
- **Incident Documentation**: Securely record officer badge numbers, patrol vehicle registrations, timestamps, and locations.
- **Audio & Media Attachment**: Voice memo recording and photo capture stored locally for legal proceedings.

## 🏛️ Legal Frameworks Referenced

| Statute / Code | Scope & Application in CIVIC |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Constitution of India** | Articles 14, 19, 20(3) (Right against self-incrimination), 21 (Life & Personal Liberty), 22 (Protection against arrest/detention), 32 & 226 (Writ remedies). |
| **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023** | Procedural criminal law replacing CrPC 1973; arrest protocols (Sec 35-43), Zero FIR (Sec 173), bail, forensic search requirements. |
| **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023** | Substantive penal code replacing IPC 1860; definition of offenses, criminal penalties, cyber offenses. |
| **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023** | Law of evidence replacing IEA 1872; electronic and digital record admissibility guidelines (Sec 61-63). |
| **Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 & Amendment 2019** | Citizen driving rights, electronic document verification (DigiLocker/mParivahan), breathalyzer regulations. |
| **Landmark Supreme Court Rulings** | *D.K. Basu* (arrest procedures), *Arnesh Kumar* (arrest necessity), *K.S. Puttaswamy* (right to privacy), *Lalita Kumari* (mandatory FIR registration). |

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- [Flutter SDK](https://docs.flutter.dev/get-started/install) (version 3.13.2 or later)
- [Dart SDK](https://dart.dev/get-dart) (version 3.1.0 or later)
- Google Chrome or Chromium (for web preview)
- Android Studio / SDK (for Android builds)
- [Firebase CLI](https://firebase.google.com/docs/cli) (for deployment & Data Connect)

### Setup & Local Development

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Rishit-07/Civic.git
   cd civic
   ```

2. **Install project dependencies:**
   ```bash
   flutter pub get
   ```

3. **Run locally:**
   - **Web (Chrome):**
     ```bash
     flutter run -d chrome
     ```
   - **Android:**
     ```bash
     flutter run -d <device-id>
     ```
   - **Windows:**
     ```bash
     flutter run -d windows
     ```

### Testing

Execute unit and widget tests:

```bash
flutter test
```
